# Pesticides and Hypospadias in Southern Brazil: an Ecologic Study

## Overview  
This repository contains data and analysis from the study titled **"Pesticides and Hypospadias in Southern Brazil: an Ecologic Study"**. The study investigates the association between environmental exposure to pesticides and the risk of hypospadias in municipalities of Rio Grande do Sul, a Brazilian state characterized by intensive agricultural activity and pesticide use.  

## Methods  

- **Study Design**:  
  A population-based ecological study was conducted, encompassing 1.8 million live births from 2010 to 2022.  

- **Spatial Modeling**:  
  A Bayesian spatial hierarchical model (BYM2) was applied to estimate relative risks of hypospadias across municipalities, accounting for spatial dependence and heterogeneity.  

- **Exposure Proxies**:  
  - **Agricultural Land Use**:  
    Municipal-level data on land area dedicated to specific crops (e.g., soybean, rice, tobacco, wheat).  
  - **Pesticide Sales**:  
    Data on the volume of pesticides sold.  
  - **Pesticides detected in Water Samples**:  
    Data on the probability of detecting pesticides in water samples collected across municipalities.  

- **Outcome Data**:  
  The prevalence of hypospadias was calculated based on the total number of live births and reported cases in each municipality.
